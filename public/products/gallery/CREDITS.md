# Gallery photo credits

Freely-licensed photography from Wikimedia Commons' Unsplash imports, used as
**development placeholders** for the seeded catalog. These are photos of other
makers' shoes standing in for product photography that does not exist yet — a
shop selling real stock should replace them from the admin product form before
going public.

They are not extra angles on the seeded product. A gallery here shows several
different shoes, which is enough to exercise the detail-page gallery but is not
what real product photography would look like.

Licences are as Commons recorded them at download. Regenerate the photos with
`scripts/fetch-gallery-photos.py`; `rejected.json` beside this file remembers
source images a human rejected, so a re-run does not fetch them back.
